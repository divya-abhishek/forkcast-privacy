Forkcast Privacy Policy

Developer: wonderkinet · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Forkcast is an Android app that helps you track what is left to spend on food and plan what to cook. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the developer does not collect your data.

What stays on your phone

Your budget, spending, pantry, meal history, settings and progress are stored only on your device. The app works offline and has no account or sign-in. We do not receive, see or store this information on any server.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Forkcast has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting service of its own, and it does not share data with third parties.

The free trial and the one-time unlock

Forkcast is free for 14 days. After that, some features need a one-time purchase. To make the trial fair and to bring your purchase back on a new phone, the app keeps one small record: the date the trial started and whether you bought the unlock. This record is:

  • kept on your device;
  • copied by Android Backup to your own Google account, if you have backup turned on; and
  • stored with Google Play services (Block Store), so a reinstall does not restart the trial.

It contains no name, email address, budget or spending information. These services are run by Google under Google’s own privacy terms. You can delete the record by turning off backup and clearing the app’s data.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Forkcast never sees your card or payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase was made.

Permissions

  • Notifications, alarms and foreground service: cooking timers and gentle reminders, which you can switch off.
  • Boot completed: restores your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate and wake lock: timer alerts and haptic feedback.
  • Network access: added by Google’s Play libraries. Forkcast’s own code does not send your data anywhere.

Children

Forkcast is meant for people aged 18 and over because it deals with budgets. It is not directed at children.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Settings lets you reset your data. Because the developer holds no copy of your information, there is nothing to request or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
